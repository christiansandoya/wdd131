Lima, Peru

Peru is a country in South America with a rich history, diverse culture, and beautiful landscapes. It is home to the Andes Mountains, the Amazon rainforest, and a long Pacific coastline. Peru is also famous for its ancient civilizations, especially the Inca Empire, as well as its traditional music, dances, and delicious food. The Peruvian flag is one of the country's most important national symbols. It has three vertical stripes: two red stripes on the sides and a white stripe in the middle. The red represents the courage and sacrifice of the Peruvian people, while the white represents peace, purity, and justice. The flag reflects Peru's history, identity, and the pride that Peruvians have for their country.
