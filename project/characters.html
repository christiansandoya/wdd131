The Jedi

The Jedi are the guardians of peace and justice in the galaxy. They are skilled in the use of the Force and lightsabers, and they serve as protectors of the Galactic Republic. Guided by the Jedi Code, they value knowledge, self-discipline, compassion, and the preservation of life. During the Clone Wars, many Jedi become generals in the Republic's army, leading clone troopers into battles against the Separatist forces. As the war continues, they face difficult decisions that challenge their beliefs and their commitment to peace.

The Sith

The Sith are followers of the dark side of the Force who oppose the Jedi and seek to gain power and control over the galaxy. Unlike the Jedi, who emphasize discipline and selflessness, the Sith embrace their passions, ambition, anger, and desire for power. They use these emotions to strengthen their connection to the dark side and often manipulate others to achieve their goals. During the Clone Wars, Darth Sidious secretly orchestrates the conflict between the Galactic Republic and the Separatists, using both sides to advance his plans. His apprentice, Count Dooku, leads the Separatist movement, while other Sith and dark-side agents help carry out their schemes.
